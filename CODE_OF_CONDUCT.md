# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in DrawArch a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Disagreement should focus on ideas and evidence, never people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information. Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact channel listed in the repository. Maintainers will review reports promptly and protect reporter privacy as far as practical.
